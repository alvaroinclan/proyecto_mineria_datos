About Dataset
Overview
The Smartphone Addiction Prediction Data is a structured behavioral dataset containing over 7,500 records, focused on analyzing smartphone usage patterns and their relationship with addiction risk. It includes key variables such as screen time, app usage behavior, sleep duration, stress levels, and the impact of mobile use on daily activities and productivity.
The dataset is designed to help researchers and data scientists explore digital behavior trends and identify factors contributing to smartphone dependency. It supports tasks such as classification, pattern analysis, and predictive modeling, making it suitable for applications in digital health, user behavior analytics, and AI-driven insights.
Overall, it provides a practical foundation for understanding how modern smartphone usage influences lifestyle, mental well-being, and addiction tendencies.

Dataset Application
Build machine learning models to predict smartphone addiction risk using behavioral data.
Analyze the impact of screen time on sleep patterns, stress levels, and mental well-being.
Study user behavior trends and daily smartphone usage habits for research purposes.
Develop AI-based systems that recommend healthier screen-time and digital habits.
Identify high-risk individuals for early intervention and prevention strategies.
Support academic research in data science, psychology, and human-computer interaction.
Evaluate the effect of smartphone usage on productivity, focus, and daily activities.
Perform classification and pattern analysis for addiction level segmentation.
